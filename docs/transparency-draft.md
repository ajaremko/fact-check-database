Fact-Check Archive: Bot Transparency & Data Compliance PolicyLast Updated: July 2026Welcome to the Fact-Check Archive. We are a free, public-interest historical archive dedicated to indexing global fact-checking data to combat misinformation. We believe in being a responsible internet citizen and operate our automated data collection with strict adherence to web standards, publisher rights, and ethical scraping practices.1. Identification and TransparencyOur automated indexer identifies itself transparently on every network request. We never mask our IP addresses, use proxy rotating networks to bypass security, or spoof browser headers.User-Agent String: FactCheckArchiveBot/1.0 (+https://yourdomain.com)Intent: Our bot exclusively requests public RSS feeds and index pages to extract factual metadata (claims, dates, and verdicts).2. Respect for Webmaster ControlsWe strictly honor the rules set by content creators and website administrators:Robots.txt: Our bot checks and obeys robots.txt directives before crawling any domain. If your site blocks FactCheckArchiveBot or universally restricts automated access, we will not index your feed.Crawl Delay: We implement a mandatory rate-limiting delay between requests to ensure we do not strain host servers or impact website performance.3. Copyright, Attribution, and Linking PolicyOur archive is designed to act as an index, not a replacement for original journalism.No Content Hosting: We do not scrape, store, or republish full-text articles or analysis paragraphs.No Image Hotlinking: We do not render, copy, or hotlink original images, graphics, or logos.Direct Attribution: Every indexed fact-check features a prominent, clickable hyperlink routing users directly to the original publisher’s website so they receive full traffic and ad revenue.4. Publisher Opt-Out and Takedown RequestsIf you are a publisher and wish to have your indexed links removed from our public archive, or if you want to block our bot entirely, we provide an immediate, no-questions-asked opt-out process.Please email your request to: compliance@yourdomain.comAlternatively, update your robots.txt to include:
User-agent: FactCheckArchiveBot
Disallow: /
